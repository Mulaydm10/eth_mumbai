## VERDICTS

### S-B Tremor Witness: REAL (narrow, locally costly); "disputed contractor data" NOT evidenced
Stated pain: Colaba residents demand "installed vibration monitoring instruments with real-time reporting shared with residents" plus independent audit, already in HC and SC (FPJ 23 May 2025) [FETCHED]. Elphinstone ~50 residents (14 Nov 2025) [FETCHED]. Mahim buildings tilted, private audit "beyond repair"; SC ordered MMRCL to "file details pertaining to any seismic movement or tremors" [FETCHED].
Who/how many: Metro-3 Kalbadevi-Girgaon 26 buildings / 737 families (2015) [SNIPPET]; city-wide unsized.
Cost: no rupee figure found; unsized.
Today: contractor/authority monitoring (Elphinstone, within one day); court petitions; private structural audits.
Switching: demand for independent monitors is stated; but phone-accelerometer PPV is weak evidence vs calibrated geophones, and a hired auditor is the incumbent.
Limits: DIN 4150-3 4-20 mm/s residential, 3-10 mm/s sensitive [FETCHED vendor]; Indian limit unfound.
Resistance: authorities/contractors (data ownership, liability); courts' evidentiary standards.
Against: complaints are "no data shared", not "data forged"; authority reacted fast; blockchain adds little over a timestamped independent log.
Opening line: Colaba residents demanded "vibration monitoring instruments with real-time reporting shared with residents" (FPJ, 23 May 2025) https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/mumbai/mumbai-news-heritage-wall-near-gateway-of-india-develops-cracks-after-colaba-jetty-drilling-residents-demand-structural-audit-and-vibration-monitoring . Alternative: SC "We direct MMRCL to file details pertaining to any seismic movement or tremors" (mumbailive link above).
Can't they just: hire a structural auditor / court-ordered third-party monitor (VJTI at Elphinstone).

### S-C Hum Clock: NICE-TO-HAVE for the stated bottleneck
Fraud is real (Verisk: 99% of insurers saw manipulated media) but no source shows "when was it filmed" is the bottleneck; reuse is handled by cross-insurer image matching [SNIPPET]; insurers can mandate controlled capture (Truepic/C2PA) cheaply. ENF works only on clips with mains hum/flicker. India photo-fraud figure: unsized (only 2019 unsourced 10-13% claims-fraud figure).
Opening: "99% of insurers have encountered manipulated or AI-altered claim documentation; only 58% are very confident detecting edits to real photos" https://amp.claimsjournal.com/news/national/2026/03/17/336303.htm
Can't they just: require in-app controlled capture (Truepic/C2PA) or a fresh live photo.

### S-D Pocket Sonar: REAL & COSTLY as health problem (undiagnosed OSA); product-problem fit not evidenced
Over 85% of ~33M Indian working adults with OSA undiagnosed (modelled; community studies suggest lower prevalence, 3.5% [SNIPPET]). 5.7% of 60+ live alone [FETCHED]. Sleep study cost Rs 1,700-20,000 [SNIPPET]. Prior art: UW ApneaApp 2015, 95-99% on 37 patients [FETCHED], never became a product. A breathing pause alarm risks false alarms; screening and elderly-alarm are different needs; CDSCO/liability.
Opening: "Over 85% of an estimated 33 million Indian working adults with sleep apnoea are undiagnosed" (modelled estimate) https://www.ispor.org/heor-resources/presentations-database/presentation-cti/ispor-europe-2025/poster-session-4-2/quantifying-the-economic-burden-of-undiagnosed-sleep-apnea-in-india-s-productive-population-a-health-economic-modeling-approach
Can't they just: Rs 2,000-5,000 home sleep test, or SpO2 wearable / motion-sensor elder monitors.

### E3 MachineLease: REAL BUT MINOR / UNPROVEN in India
Only vendor claims ("5 to 15 percent of manually tracked rental invoices contain an hour discrepancy", FleetRabbit) and one Indian allegation (Bhiwandi, Sep 2026). Telematics (OEM and aftermarket) already gives run/idle time. Crypto settlement adds friction. India dispute scale: unsized.
Opening (weak, vendor): https://fleetrabbit.com/industry/construction-management-system/construction-fleet-reducing-equipment-hours-disputes-rentals
Can't they just: fit GPS/engine-hour telematics or read the OEM feed.
