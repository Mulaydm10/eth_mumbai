## IDEAS (generation mode; criticism off)

### 1. Neighbour Seismograph: a tamper-evident, multi-phone vibration witness for construction disputes
- Problem: residents of old Mumbai buildings next to metro/flyover piling cannot prove how hard their walls were shaken, because the only vibration meters belong to the contractor/authority. Who suffers: residents, small societies.
- Evidence: URLs in search 20 (Elphinstone, Sewri-Worli, Metro-3 Mahim/Dadar).
- Mechanism: each phone lying on a wall/floor logs accelerometer (PPV in mm/s, dominant frequency; piling is ~impulsive) and records a short mic burst. Phones in different flats are time-aligned by an ultrasonic sync chirp (cross-correlation to sub-ms) and then the arrival-time differences of the piling impact across floors/buildings give a TDOA direction/distance estimate to the source. A quorum rule (cf. MyShake: >=4 phones and >60% in 10 km agree) rejects one person shaking a phone. Each confirmed event = an Arkiv entity {site, ppv_mm_s, f_dom, tdoa_bearing, quorum_n} with a TTL set to the limitation/complaint window; entities are queryable by attribute and $expiration.
- Ingredients: MyShake quorum rule https://news.berkeley.edu/2016/02/12/n.. ; phone TDOA (Freiburg) https://forum.cone.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/pubs/IPIN2010_extended_abstract.pdf ; Arkiv API https://arkiv.dev.golem.network/docs/api ; Elphinstone evidence above.
- Sponsor stack: Arkiv (TTL entities + attribute query ARE the evidence store; the retention window is a legal concept) + ENSv2 (one name per building/site, per-name EAC roles: "contributor" role for flat-phones, "auditor" role for the society, so each phone writes only its own readings; resolvers publish the site's latest summary). Optional 3rd: Anon-RPC so a resident checking readings is not identified to a contractor-linked RPC.
- Phone role: central. Browser: Generic Sensor Accelerometer works in Android Chrome but is rate-capped (~60 Hz by default; UNVERIFIED by me) so PPV below ~25 Hz only; audio via Web Audio works. A browser-only version is plausible for the low-frequency band; native app would give 200+ Hz.
- Level-3 component: cross-phone time alignment by ultrasonic chirp matched filter plus quorum/TDOA event localisation, with measured sync error and bearing error.
- WOW: three phones on the demo table, tap a mallet; the page draws the bearing to the tap and the sync error in ms; a lone phone shaken by hand is rejected by quorum; Arkiv query shows the entity expiring.
- Self-measurable number: inter-phone clock sync error (ms) and TDOA bearing error (degrees) over 20 mallet taps; PPV vs a reference (e.g. a known calibration shake table substitute: phone on a washing machine/speaker at a known frequency).

### 2. Grid-Clock Notary: when was this video really recorded
- Problem: photo/video evidence (damage claims, delivery or inspection proofs, monsoon-flood claims) is easy to replay or backdate; metadata is editable. Who suffers: insurers, claimants with honest evidence, platforms.
- Evidence: mechanism literature only (no incident stats found). Rolling-shutter ENF forensics https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1903.09889 .
- Mechanism: mains-powered lights flicker at 2x grid frequency; the grid frequency wanders around 50 Hz by tens of mHz. A rolling-shutter camera samples that flicker row by row (my calc: ~32 kHz row rate at 30 fps x 1080 rows). Extract the ENF trace from the clip with a band-pass + STFT/phase tracker, then correlate against a reference ENF trace recorded by "witness phones" (mic hum at 100 Hz or magnetometer at 50 Hz) plugged near mains. The best correlation lag gives the recording time; a replayed older clip matches an old time, an edited clip has breaks. Reference traces live as Arkiv entities (attribute: minute, ENF samples) with a rolling TTL, so the reference ledger is self-pruning.
- Ingredients: ENF rolling-shutter papers (above); Arkiv TTL/query https://arkiv.dev.golem.network/docs/api ; Android Generic Sensor / getUserMedia in browser.
- Sponsor stack: Arkiv (reference ENF windows as TTL entities; this is a natural fit for sliding-window data), plus EF CROPS (needs no trusted timestamp server; open reference network) or ENSv2 (witness-phone names with a "reference-writer" role). 
- Phone: both roles (witness and subject). Browser: video frames to canvas gives row means; feasible in Chrome Android, but frame-rate/idle-gap handling is the hard part.
- Honest caveat to carry into later stages: India is one synchronous grid for most regions, so ENF proves TIME, not place; also needs mains-lit scene.
- Level-3 component: ENF extraction from rolling-shutter video with idle-period correction, with a measured time-localisation error and false-match rate.
- WOW: film a lit lamp for 20 s; page prints "recorded 14:02:31 +-2 s, matches witness ledger"; then play a recording of the same lamp from 10 minutes ago and it says "10 min earlier"; edit-splice shows a break.
- Number: time error (s) over 30 clips; false-match rate versus random reference offsets; minimum clip length for 95% correct localisation.

### 3. Pocket Sonar Sentinel: breath-pause alarm with a safe-stop AI in the loop
- Problem: breathing pauses (sleep apnea, infant/elder monitoring) go unnoticed; wearables are costly and uncomfortable. Who suffers: caregivers, undiagnosed sleepers.
- Evidence: ApneaApp clinical study (37 patients, 296 h) https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~gshyam/Papers/apneaapp.pdf . Prevalence stats not searched.
- Mechanism: phone speaker emits an 18-22 kHz FMCW chirp (B=4 kHz gives 4.3 cm range resolution, computed), mic de-chirps by mixing, range-FFT, CFAR picks the chest range bin, phase of that bin over slow time gives the breathing waveform; Doppler gives gross motion. A 0G-hosted classifier makes the alarm decision; if 0G is unreachable the device enters a declared safe state (local conservative alarm threshold) and says so.
- Ingredients: ApneaApp (above); ggwave shows browser ultrasound is possible https://github.com/ggerganov/ggwave ; 0G Physical AI track rules (from context file).
- Sponsor stack: 0G (Physical AI: pocket device, inference in the loop with safe stop; this is exactly the rubric) + ENSv2 (caregiver gets a per-record "read alerts" EAC role on the sleeper's name, revocable) . Only these two are needed. NOTE: guardian/delegation framing is close to OFF-LIST "guardians"; keep roles as a supporting feature.
- Phone: central, works in a browser (Web Audio chirp + getUserMedia with echo cancellation off; speaker/mic response above 18 kHz varies by device).
- Level-3 component: on-phone FMCW range-Doppler pipeline with CFAR chest tracking and breath-pause detector.
- WOW: put the phone on the table, a teammate breathes; live range-bin plot shows the chest; hold breath 10 s, alarm; pull the network, UI flips to "SAFE STOP: no decisions faked".
- Number: breaths-per-minute MAE vs manual count at 0.5/1.0/1.5 m; apnea-event detection recall and false alarms per hour on a 1-hour overnight recording.

### 4. Curtailment Meter: prove an AC was switched off with a phone magnetometer
- Problem: paying households to cut load in a grid peak needs measurement and verification, but homes have no interval meter/ no access to one. Who suffers: DISCOMs, aggregators, households who do not get paid.
- Evidence: mechanism only; patent US9869705 https://patents.justia.com/patent/20140266177 ; AMagScope accuracy 20-50% absolute (see search 18). No Indian demand-response stats searched (gap).
- Mechanism: magnetic field around the supply cable is at mains frequency and proportional to current. Absolute accuracy is poor, but a before/after RATIO of the 50 Hz line amplitude (lock-in demodulation, sub-Nyquist alias recovery because phone magnetometers sample ~50-100 Hz) detects an AC compressor going off with far better reliability. The signed event "phone clamped to cable X saw a >80% drop for 15 min" is a measurement, plus the phone's own ENF-style time anchor.
- Sponsor stack: Whitechain (gasless payout to the household, "payments nobody notices") + EF frame transactions (verify-frame checks the attestation, sponsor-frame pays gas, execute-frame pays out; native gas sponsorship is a documented EIP-8141 feature https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8141-frame-transaction/27617 ; details of the mempool policy unverified). Maybe Arkiv for events with TTL.
- Phone: central; Web Generic Sensor Magnetometer in Android Chrome (rate cap unverified).
- Level-3: lock-in/alias-recovery detector with a measured ROC for compressor on/off events.
- WOW: phone strapped on a table fan/kettle cord; toggle the appliance; live line amplitude collapses, a payout lands on a phone without gas, shown on screen.
- Number: detection probability and false-alarm rate over 50 on/off toggles of a kettle/fan/AC; ratio error vs a plug-in power meter if available.

### 5. Milk-and-Oil Shake Test: a phone's own vibration motor as a viscometer
- Problem: milk and edible oil in India are routinely diluted/blended and consumers cannot tell. Who suffers: households, honest kirana/dairy sellers.
- Evidence: FSSAI milk survey, 68.4% of 1,791 samples non-conforming (2011) https://dairyreporter.com/Article/2012/10/22/Majority-of-Indian-milk-violates-standards-government ; edible oil 24.2% failed quality metrics https://foodsafetyworks.com/news/24-edible-oil-samples-adulterated-fssai/ .
- Mechanism: place the phone against a sealed cup; run vibration motor and read accelerometer; damping and response spectrum shift with viscosity/density (Vi-Liquid: ~97% over 30 liquids, viscosity MRE 2.3%, per slides) https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28787 . Tied to a batch via an attestation of (liquid class, damping coefficient, phone model).
- Sponsor stack: Arkiv (per-batch test entities with TTL = shelf life, queryable by shop/batch) + ENSv2 (a shop's name carries "lab" and "seller" roles so only the seller may publish and only a certified tester may attest). 
- Phone: central; browser can call navigator.vibrate but the motor/accelerometer sampling coupling is weaker in the browser (accelerometer rate cap) so native is preferred.
- Level-3: damping-coefficient estimator + classifier with measured accuracy over water-dilution levels.
- WOW: three identical cups (pure, 10%, 30% water) are classified live in front of the judges, blind.
- Number: accuracy/confusion matrix over 5 dilution levels x 10 trials; detection limit in % water.
- Weakness to flag for later: the on-chain part is thin; the sensing part is a replication of published work.

### 8. Verify-then-Sponsor Frames: a protocol where gas is paid only if a physical proof checks out (pure protocol, no phone required)
- Problem: gasless relays are abused or must trust the relayer; "payments nobody notices" need a payer who pays for gas only for valid, evidenced actions. Who suffers: sponsors burning gas on spam; users blocked by gas.
- Mechanism: use a frame transaction where frame 1 (VERIFY) runs custom validation logic over an evidence commitment (e.g., ENF-matched or quorum-signed event from ideas 1/2/4), frame 2 (SPONSOR) pays gas only if frame 1 succeeded, frame 3 executes. Measurable: gas wasted by sponsor under spam attack versus a 4337 paymaster baseline.
- Ingredients: EIP-8141 discussion https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/eip-8141-frame-transaction/27617 ; frame mempool note https://hackmd.io/@matt/frame-mempool . My search suggests frames run in the public mempool, not an "alternative" mempool; track statement in the context file may differ (need to check the actual EF track text).
- Sponsor stack: EF frame-transaction track + Whitechain gasless + Arkiv. 
- Phone role: none required; consumer of ideas 1/2/4 as evidence sources.
- Level-3: validation-frame design with a measured spam-resistance (gas wasted per 1000 invalid submissions).
- WOW: spam run of 1,000 invalid claims costs the sponsor 0 gas in the frame version, X in the baseline.
- Number: sponsor gas lost per 1,000 invalid submissions, frame vs baseline.

## DISCARDED IDEAS (with reasons)
1. Water-tanker volume by acoustic resonance in the tank mouth. Mechanism is sound (echo/resonance gives air-column length at cm resolution) but I found NO evidence that short delivery is the actual dispute (search 19 shows price gouging and strikes, not volume fraud). No demonstrable problem.
2. Crowd density on Mumbai local trains from IMU/barometer. M-Loc gives station localisation (93%/3 stations) not crowd count https://arxiv.org/pdf/2003.10531 ; carriage-level sensing unproven; and no crypto necessity.
3. Anon-RPC medicine recall lookup (Arkiv recall entities, private reads). Needed? Headline too close to OFF-LIST "RPC-read privacy leak meters" and the problem is integration-heavy with no Level-3 physical part.
4. Frequency-aware SwapVM/hook that widens spread when grid frequency deviates. Contrived; no evidence of demand; I could not verify SwapVM docs at all (search 14).
5. Wi-Fi RTT relay-resistance proof for payments. FTM can be shifted by tens of metres by attackers (survey https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.03901), and tap-to-pay/proximity payments are OFF-LIST/Enconomy-adjacent.
6. Vibration-motor covert channel for offline phone-to-phone key transfer (2.5-5 bps, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4177823/). Nice trick but resembles the offline-transaction items on the OFF-LIST and has no problem owner.
7. Phone earthquake early warning (MyShake-like). Already a deployed product; no incremental technical edge for hackathon; folded into idea 1 as the quorum rule.
8. Phone-as-multi-mic gunshot/leak localisation. Two or three mics ambiguous, reverberation, no evidence found (search 11).
9. Magnetometer-based absolute energy billing. Accuracy 20-50% (AMagScope) makes absolute billing indefensible; only the ratio trick survived into idea 4.
