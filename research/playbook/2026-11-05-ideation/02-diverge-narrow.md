## 1. LIFEBOAT: rescue a drained wallet inside ONE transaction
- **User:** a person whose private key leaked (seed phished, malicious Permit signed) and who sees a sweeper bot eating every ETH they deposit for gas.
- **Problem (plain words):** "My wallet is being drained, and the money I send to pay for the rescue is stolen before I can use it."
- **Evidence:** Scam Sniffer 2025: $83.85M, 106,106 victims; Permit signatures 38% of >$1M losses; $908K USDC taken 458 days after a forgotten approval [E] https://drops.scamsniffer.io/?p=1115 , https://www.bitdegree.org/crypto/news/forgotten-wallet-approval-lets-scammer-drain-over-900000-in-usdc . Sweeper bots sweep gas deposits from the public mempool; EIP-7702 bots can self-sponsor [E] https://support.metamask.io/stay-safe/protect-yourself/fighting-back-against-sweeper-bots/ , https://avaprotocol.org/blog/eip-7702-rescue-gasaccount-swept
- **Today:** Flashbots-bundle rescue by a technical friend (Ethereum-only, needs a builder relationship, fragile), or "move to a new wallet" and accept the loss; Revoke.cash needs gas the victim cannot safely hold.
- **Mechanism:** one frame transaction: frame 1 verify (victim key approves execution), frame 2 verify (a rescue sponsor approves paying gas, so the victim holds zero ETH), then an atomic-batch of SENDER frames: revoke all approvals + move assets to a fresh key. It goes to an alternative mempool so the bot never sees a gas-deposit step. A phone holds the fresh key (Android Keystore).
- **Ingredients:** EIP-8141 https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8141 (payer separate from sender; atomic-batch flag; CFI for Hegota) ; 4pillars explainer https://4pillars.io/en/issues/frame-transactions-setting-ethereum-transaction-free ; Anon-RPC https://ethereum.github.io/anon-rpc/ (supporting only: lets the victim read their own approvals without telling the RPC which address is in trouble).
- **Sponsor stack:** EF Frame-Tx Alternative Mempool (the one EF prize; NEEDED because the whole point is a sponsor-paid atomic rescue that no public-mempool deposit can be front-run). Optional 2nd: ENS (ENSv2 role revocation so the compromised key loses control of the victim's name, since a leaked key often owns the name too) [U]. No third.
- **Phone role:** holds the new key; one-hand "panic" (shake detected by IMU) starts the rescue; shows the live bot race.
- **Level-3 component:** the rescue path itself, proven against a sweeper bot WE write (Dhruv's attack-search background): atomic sponsor+revoke+sweep that wins the race against a live bot, including the self-sponsoring EIP-7702 bot variant.
- **WOW (4 min):** a judge sends 0.01 test ETH to the "victim" address; our bot sweeps it in seconds (classic failure shown live); then the same victim runs LIFEBOAT with zero ETH and everything lands in the new address in one block.
- **Self-measured number:** rescue success rate over N=100 seeded bot races (naive deposit-then-act vs frame-tx), plus value saved and blocks-to-safety.
- **Risk to check later (not a filter):** whether any frame-tx devnet / alt-mempool endpoint exists to deploy against is UNVERIFIED by me.
