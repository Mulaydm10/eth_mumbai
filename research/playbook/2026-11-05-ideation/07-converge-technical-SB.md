## FINDINGS (severity-tagged)

HIGH-1 Browser 60 Hz cap degrades the headline PPV. Evidence [6][7][9]: Chromium `SensorTraits<>::kMaxAllowedFrequency = 60.0` clamps Accelerometer and LinearAccelerationSensor (and DeviceMotion pump is 60 Hz). At 60 Hz Nyquist is 30 Hz, the sensor's internal LPF is lower, and piling at 10-25 Hz plus impact harmonics sits at/over the edge: in my sim a taped phone at 5-10 mm/s reads -25..-30% on impacts (p10..p90 -44..-11%) and +13..+17% on a 20 Hz burst; at 200 Hz the same cases are within about -5..+3%. A native Android app (200 Hz without permission since Android 12; more with HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS) fixes the bandwidth. Recommendation: PWA = discovery/quorum/UI with "indicative" labelling; native or Capacitor/TWA-with-native-plugin for the PPV claim. Sure: medium-high (cap = source-verified; error = my model).
HIGH-2 Coupling dominates and cannot be calibrated. [9] Phone loose on tile (30 Hz resonance, 0.8 gain): 20 Hz burst PPV reads +29..+34% at 200/400 Hz, +48% at 60 Hz, +60% at 1 mm/s; impacts +6..+8% (200 Hz) but -19% (60 Hz). Hand-set model, direction/size depend on the resonance; the point is a tens-of-percent unknown, comparable to the metro authority's "cannot be that accurate" objection. Mitigation: require taped/wall-mounted placement, report as a band (e.g. +/-30%), compare relative level across phones, not absolute vs limit. Sure: medium (model).
HIGH-3 TDOA bearing is not real with phone clocks in different flats [16]: delays 1.7-500 ms vs clock error 10-100+ ms (SNTP ~daily, up to 2.5 s theoretical) and 16.7 ms sample quantisation at 60 Hz. Recommendation: DROP TDOA; keep quorum + time-window coincidence (T = 3-5 s), and amplitude-ratio ranking between phones ("loudest side of the building") if a direction cue is wanted. Sure: high (arithmetic), medium (clock magnitudes).
HIGH-4 ENSv2 EAC cannot give "resident writes only its own sensor's records on the building name" in one grant [15]: PermissionedResolver roles are scoped per KEY (keccak256(key)) not per name; "There is no per-name scoping"; names that need separation need their own resolver instance (or per-sensor key names). grantSetterRoles(setter, account) with ROLE_SET_TEXT exists and works per key. Also text records are an awkward store for PPV; Arkiv is the right layer. Sure: medium-high.
HIGH-5 One-human-one-sensor cannot be enforced from a PWA [11][12]: World AgentKit proves "a unique verified human registered this wallet" (World App flow + World Chain registration), with no device/sensor attestation; Android Key Attestation/Play Integrity are native-only APIs, WebAuthn conveyance defaults to "none" and no source shows Chrome-Android returning android-key attestation. So a single human can run many scripted "phones" via synthetic readings; quorum of humans (World ID) bounds it to one vote per human, but not "that phone physically vibrated". Honest claim: "N distinct verified humans signed matching readings in a T-second window", not "N sensors". Sure: medium (negative evidence).
MED-1 Low-level events are noise-limited [9]: 1 mm/s at 5-10 Hz is 3-6 mg vs 0.9-2.3 mg RMS (160 ug/rtHz) or 0.4-1.0 mg (70 ug/rtHz); PPV bias +15..+35% at 1 mm/s. 3-10 mm/s at 10-40 Hz (19-256 mg) is 10-100x above noise and fine. DIN/IS limits of interest (2-5 mm/s sensitive/dwelling at low f) are right at the edge for the noisy IMU class (BMI260 160 ug/rtHz) and fine for 70 ug/rtHz parts. Sure: medium-high.
MED-2 No quotable measured smartphone-vs-reference error obtained [5]: found only qualitative "acceptable accuracy" (HK CIC shaking-table), an ISEE 2018 blast comparison (abstract only), and E&MJ evidence that accelerometer-integrated PPV can exceed geophone PPV (~2x in one example, high-frequency content). Caller should not quote a % from the literature; use own shake-table/handheld-vs-meter test if available. Sure: low on the number, high that it was not found.
MED-3 E1 Anon-RPC: browser-only published package; reads work through `worker.fetch` to any HTTP JSON-RPC URL; real anonymization only via tor-js/nym/nox workers (need gateways, 18 MB bundle, seconds of bootstrap); latency not published and I could not measure it (Node harness unpublished, passthrough-only, not conforming). E1's "before/after latency" would have to be measured in a browser by the team. Sure: high.
LOW-1 Arkiv: "Anyone can write an entity a query matches" (README): the dashboard must filter on $owner/$creator or a schema tag; Rabdi RPC rate-limited without access key (S2 notes). Sure: high.
LOW-2 DIN 4150-3 numerics not verified from the standard here (only secondary hits; values in [4] are from memory); Indian limits from secondary summaries; limits are for short-term/blast, piling is continuous/repeated -> use the standard's lower, "long-term" values for the claim. Sure: low-medium.
