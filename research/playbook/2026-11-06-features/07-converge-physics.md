## Entry 3: sound-sync detector simulation (scratch /private/tmp/claude-501/pb-c3/sync_sim.ts; imports real ToneSyncDetector)
- Method: synthesised 3-tone (3/5/7 kHz, 90 ms, 4 ms ramps) at 48 kHz into noise (SNR 30/15/6 dB), random onset phase vs buffer grid, fed in exactly as sound.ts does (2048-sample buffer, 256-sample blocks, block start = callback time - bufMs + i/fs), callback delivery delay modelled as zero-latency + half-normal (sd 0/3/8 ms) or exponential (mean 8/20 ms). 400 trials each.
- Result: detector+quantisation floor (zero callback jitter): mean bias -1 to +1.4 ms, sd 1.5-1.6 ms, any SNR 6-30 dB (so the block quantisation and Goertzel threshold are NOT the problem). Error then equals the callback delivery delay: half-normal sd 3 ms -> bias +1.6..3.8, sd 2.3-2.5; sd 8 -> bias ~5-8, sd ~4.8; exponential mean 8 ms -> bias ~7-10, sd 8-9; exponential mean 20 ms -> bias ~18-20, sd 18-21. Failure to detect <1% at 6 dB.
- Meaning: the sync error is dominated by (i) ScriptProcessor callback delivery delay dated as zero (bias common to all phones cancels in alignBySound's median only if every phone has the same delay distribution; the per-phone random part does not cancel) and (ii) per-device input (mic) latency, which is a constant per device model not modelled at all and not measured by the code. Both are outside what the code can see; the simulated sd for plausible 3-8 ms jitter is 2.5-5 ms, for a busy main thread 9-20 ms. These are ASSUMED jitter numbers - the real jitter on Chrome/Android must be measured (EXP-0002 step). Confidence: medium on the detector floor (computed), low on real-world jitter (assumption).

## Entry 5: amplitude-consistency collusion simulation (scratch rank_sim.py, rank_sim2.py in /private/tmp/claude-501/pb-c3; numpy venv there)
Model: phones uniform in 8x8 m room, min spacing 1.5 m, declared positions known to +/-0.5 m; amplitude a_i = sum_k s_k * max(r_ik,0.3)^-p, times lognormal per-phone factor sd sig (sensitivity + coupling). H0 = one stomper at a random point; H1 = two colluders, each ~0.2 m from a random phone, strength ratio lognormal(sd 0.3). Statistic = min over p in {0.5..1.0} and over a 41x41 source grid of rms ln-residual of a point-source fit (strength free). Threshold = 95th percentile of H0 (5% false alarm, calibrated on the model itself, so optimistic). 400 trials/cell. p = true decay exponent.
TPR@5%FPR (AUC):
- sig=0.2: p=0.5 -> n3 .30 (.75), n4 .40 (.74), n5 .42 (.73); p=0.75 -> .77/.78/.75 (AUC .91-.94); p=1.0 -> .91/.91/.94 (AUC .98)
- sig=0.4: p=0.5 -> .18/.25/.18 (AUC .62-.65); p=0.75 -> .37/.33/.38 (AUC .76-.81); p=1.0 -> .64/.79/.75 (AUC .92-.93)
- sig=0.7: p=0.5 -> .12/.12/.11 (AUC ~.57); p=0.75 -> .24/.22/.12; p=1.0 -> .34/.34/.37 (AUC ~.78)
- n=3 vs 4 vs 5 barely differ (the point-source fit has 3 + p parameters; extra phones add little at this room size). More phones do NOT rescue it; noise sig and decay exponent dominate.
- ADAPTIVE colluders who tune the strength ratio to minimise the statistic: TPR@5% p=0.75: .30/.29/.30 (sig .2), .15/.10/.19 (sig .4); p=1.0: .66/.62/.71 (sig .2), .40/.22/.25 (sig .4).
- Declared position error 2 m (instead of 0.5): p=1: n4 .74(sig .2)/.63(.4), n5 .67/.57 - modest loss. Per-event decay exponent jitter +/-0.15 (p=.75, sig .2): n4 .63, n5 .69.
- Takeaway: amplitude-consistency detects a colluder pair only when the decay is steep (p ~1, near-field-ish) and phone-to-phone factors are good to ~20-40% (1 sigma). For p<=0.75 or sig>=0.4 it is a weak test (TPR 10-40% at 5% FPR) and is trivially defeated by adaptive strengths. Real floors are not point-source power laws (resonances, slab modes, walls), so this is an UPPER bound on separability. Real data's same-placement ratio sd of 0.19 (n=7) is the best-case sig for a pair; coupling differences (table vs floor) are not in the logs.
- n=2 phones: any amplitude pair fits some single source - no test possible (stated, not simulated). Without known phone positions the same applies for n<=4 (3 free source params + p).
Confidence: model-bound; high that the numbers are what the model gives, low that they transfer to a real building.

## Entry 7: D1 F3 / I-15 low-rate mode and Nyquist (scratch nyq.ts; real VelocityTracker on the 32 real structural waveforms from the 22-22-09 log)
- Arithmetic: 10 Hz sampling => Nyquist 5 Hz; 20 Hz => 10 Hz. The code's own band LP = min(25, 0.42*fs) => 4.2 Hz at 10 Hz, 8.4 Hz at 20 Hz. The advertised 2-25 Hz band needs fs >= 60 (0.42*60=25.2). So a 10-20 Hz mode (D5/narrow-legal F3) is a different, narrower band: 2-4 Hz (10 Hz) or 2-8 Hz (20 Hz), or aliases if the sensor does not low-pass. CONFIRMED conflict. Evidence the signals live above that: fDom of the real structural triggers: 16 of 32 above 10 Hz, 30 of 32 above 5 Hz, 23 of 32 above 8.4 Hz (range 2.8-20.1 Hz).
- PPV after decimating the real waveforms (ratio to full-rate PPV; min / median / max), point-decimation (alias-prone) vs block-average: x3 (~21 Hz): 0.48/0.99/1.98 point, 0.43/0.82/1.01 avg; x4 (~16 Hz): 0.43/0.98/2.37, 0.34/0.64/1.03; x7 (~9 Hz): 0.43/0.73/1.76, 0.18/0.53/0.98. So reduced rate biases PPV low (anti-aliased) or randomly high/low x0.4-2.4 (aliased). The PPV is NOT comparable across phones running different modes, and quorum thresholds in mm/s break. Caveat: input waves are already sensor-filtered/rounded 0.1 m/s^2 and ~1.3 s long. Confidence: medium-high for the conflict; the size of the bias is indicative.
