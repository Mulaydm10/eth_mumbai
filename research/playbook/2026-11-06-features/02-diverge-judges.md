## 2. JUDGE PSYCHOLOGY (4-min demo + 3-min Q&A). Evidence tags: [P]=primary ETHGlobal source above, [I]=my inference
What lands:
- Show the product, not slides: ETHGlobal's own staff: "if your demo is three minutes of slide and one minute of demo, there's really low chance... we want to actually see what you built" [P Mumbai workshop 32:16]. Interaction invited: "if people can interact with it, that is great" [P].
- A stake in under ~30 s (personal story, number, contradiction) [P Cannes/Tokyo stage demos: npmguard $60B, DIVE Kalshi-vs-Polymarket contradiction, Kura store owner, Takarabako leftover cash].
- "I learned something new" moment for the judge (Enconomy: host "didn't know you could read the echo off of your phone's microphone") [P]. Tremor Witness analogue: the judge's own phone feels the motor of a spare phone.
- Real, live, public: "199 of 254 chose finalist judging... deploy live", "more than half were live websites... all public including source code" [P]; independence of judges: senior independent ecosystem people, not ETHGlobal or partners [P].
- Audience participation at stage level: polls (Enconomy), QR for the room to open the app (PaintGlobal) [P].
- Honest limits: "Acknowledge what you didn't build and why" [secondary blog]; Hard-rule 1 of repo also says never invent.
What hurts:
- Slides-heavy demos [P]. AI-generated idea sameness: "all the AI generated ideas that we've seen in the past were really really bad and it's the same over and over" [P 19:15] (i.e., agent/x402 crowding costs Originality).

- Judges stay hours extra and talk to every team [P Cannes + Tokyo]: judges are tired late; the first 20 s and a one-line problem matter [I].
- More than 3 integrations: ETHGlobal capped partners because "projects got way way worse... use fewer integrations, but make those integrations count" [P 22:28].
- Too-long video: upload checks fail at <2 or >4 min, <720p, music detected [P].

## 5. Q&A ATTACKS (12-15) with draft honest answers. [NB]=depends on a feature not yet built
Grounded in diverge-context.md known weaknesses; answers are drafts for the team to verify.
Q1 "Phones aren't calibrated; why trust PPV numbers?" A: We do not claim geophone-grade. Phone PPV 50-370 mm/s on close stomps is a corroborated trigger record: the claim is "k independent residents' phones each felt something above threshold in the same window", not a mm/s measurement. Each reading is signed and re-computable. [NB: an optional calibration number from a shake-table / known-source A/B would strengthen; none exists.]
Q2 "Why blockchain? Postgres would do." A: The claim is "no party, including us, can edit or delete it after the fact and anyone can recompute it with our servers off". Arkiv gives per-phone-owned entities with expiry, ENS gives the committee-run registry of who counts, Swarm gives content-addressed packs. A DB in the read path puts us (or the contractor's lawyer) in control. Show the server-off moment.
Q3 "Who pays gas?" A: Today a drip server funds 0.05 tGLM per phone on testnet; that server is only in the onboarding path, not reading or verifying. Honest: mainnet needs sponsored writes or a society fund; not built. [NB for mainnet]
Q4 "Two people stomping next to their own phones - what stops a fake quorum?" A: Quorum requires k ENS-registered phones within a window; stomping next to your own phone satisfies that for the stomper's phones. Honest: coherence check separates weakly (EXP-0001), no collusion claim. Mitigations we can state: registered committee admits phones physically (a society's secretary), independent flats, plus different-location phones will see attenuation. [NB: stronger anti-collusion e.g. attenuation-with-distance consistency]
Q5 "What stops the contractor joining 5 fake phones?" A: Phones are admitted by the committee via ENS subnames (registered, revocable, expiring), so the contractor cannot self-join; the committee is a trust root. Honest: committee key is a server key today; Safe 2-of-3 designed, not done. [NB: Safe committee]
Q6 "Arkiv expires after ~182 days; disputes last years." A: Sealed evidence packs go to Swarm with the hash anchored; the Arkiv event entity (180 d) is the index, the pack is the artifact; anyone can re-anchor. Honest gap: Swarm stamps expire too (null stamp on public gateway today); long-term pinning/renewal not built. [NB: renewal / extend job]
Q7 "Who controls the committee key?" A: Today our server holds a passcode-gated key (honest). Design: Safe 2-of-3 with society roles via ENSv2 EAC. [NB]
Q8 "Is this legal evidence in India?" A: We do not claim admissibility. Under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam electronic records need certification; our contribution is an independently recomputable, timestamped record a lawyer could attach. We have not asked a lawyer. [NB: D5 legal route; no counsel consulted]
Q9 "Phone clocks differ by ~3 s." A: We built 3-tone 3/5/7 kHz sound sync to align phones to a shared instant; the window is set in site text records. Show the sync number live. (built; real-room robustness untested)
Q10 "Is it just a vibration app? What is the technical depth?" A: Band-pass 2-25 Hz, PPV by integration, STA/LTA, dominant frequency and low-frequency ratio (ground-borne vs handling); quorum is deterministic and recomputed in the browser from the pulled raw ~1.5 s 3-axis waveform.
Q11 "Real users? Did any resident or society try it?" A: Honest: no real society asked yet [NB: a live pilot / letter of intent would be the answer]. Show what is testable by the judges' own phones today.
Q12 "What if the RPC / Arkiv / gateway is down or rate-limited?" A: Store-and-forward outbox queues writes; offline viewer file verifies from a local pack; public RPC rate-limits are a known weakness.
Q13 "Does any server load-bear for reading?" A: No (Vercel is a convenience copy). Show: switch off the backend, open the single-file viewer / run verify-event.ts. (Make sure true on the day.)
Q14 "Why would a metro contractor accept this? Isn't it adversarial?" A: Residents' record, also exculpatory for the contractor ('no quorum'); a neutral committee can include the builder. Honest: no stakeholder conversation yet.
Q15 "Privacy: phones in homes, mic for sync." A: Sound sync uses 3 pure tones, mic is not recorded or uploaded; raw waveform is ~1.5 s of accelerometer only; ENS names are pseudonymous per phone. Confirm in code before saying [verify: sync.ts does not store audio].
