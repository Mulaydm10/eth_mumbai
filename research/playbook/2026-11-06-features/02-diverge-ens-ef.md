### F-01 Phone writes ONLY its own record (argument-scoped setter grant)
(1) At join, the committee calls grantSetterRoles(setText("tw.cal.<label>"), phoneAddr) so the phone itself signs and writes its own calibration/health record (noise floor, sample rate, measured clock offset, build hash) on ENS, and cannot write anything else. (2) ENS: "letting an account edit only certain text records on a name" and "delegate specific rights". (3) Native: the quorum weights/diagnostics then come from a record only that phone key can change; the committee cannot forge it without an on-chain role grant that is itself visible. (4) ~15 s: phone taps "Publish my calibration" -> tx -> record visible via resolve; second button "try to overwrite another phone's key" -> EACUnauthorizedAccountRoles revert shown on screen. (5) Per-phone calibration history with timestamps = evidence for quorum weighting; a negative-authorization test result (revert) is itself evidence. (6) Low-medium: one grant call at join (the committee already calls register), phone needs Sepolia gas (the drip exists); key naming discipline since scoping is per key per resolver, not per name; resolver-wide grants apply across names (docs L9911-9938), so keys must embed the label. (7) Yes (can be shown with simulated phones). (8) V: eth_call simulation of grantSetterRoles succeeds for committee, reverts for stranger (sources/D3-sepolia-readonly-probes.md probe 5); docs https://docs.ens.domains/ensv2/permissioned-resolver/.
