Demo vibration source

Use a spare phone that is NOT sensing: lay it on the same table as the sensor phones and tap this button. It vibrates 5 times, 4 s apart, and each pulse should confirm on the dashboard. (Android only; iPhones don't allow web vibration.)

Sound sync (works offline)

With all phones running and close together, tap Play sync sound on any one phone. Every phone hears it at the same instant, which aligns their clocks without internet.
