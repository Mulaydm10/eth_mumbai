# Reach: who uses this and how many

**Users:** residents of older Mumbai buildings next to metro tunnelling, bridge demolition and piling, organised through their **housing society**. Each society runs one site with 3–5 phones.

**Why they need it (cited):**
- Colaba, May 2025: residents demanded "installed vibration monitoring instruments with real-time reporting shared with residents" after a heritage wall cracked near jetty drilling. Two residents' groups are already in the High Court and the Supreme Court. (Free Press Journal, 23 May 2025)
- Mahim: the Supreme Court directed MMRCL to "file details pertaining to any seismic movement or tremors as a result of construction activity" after two buildings tilted. (Mumbai Live)
- Metro-3 alone affected 26 buildings and 737 families in Kalbadevi–Girgaon (MMRC release, 2015).
- Elphinstone, Nov 2025: about 50 residents protested tremors during bridge demolition; independent monitoring started only after the protest. (Free Press Journal, 14–15 Nov 2025)

**Who reads the record (the reach of the data, not just the app):**
- residents and their society committee;
- the contractor and the authority, who can check readings instead of disputing them;
- journalists and courts, with **no account or API key needed**, straight from Arkiv or the offline viewer.

**Cost to join:** an Android phone the resident already owns, and a link. There is nothing to install.

**Honest limits:**
- We have not yet signed up a real society. Our teammate's outreach is in progress, and any reply will be added here verbatim.
- We have not sized the number of affected buildings city-wide.
- Phone readings are a corroborated *trigger record*, not a calibrated geophone measurement.
