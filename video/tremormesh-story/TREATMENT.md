# TREMORMESH: The Story of Ramesh (treatment)

The structure, wording and order are fixed by `SCRIPT.md`. This version has **no voice**: every narration line has a reserved window (2.4 words/s, +0.6 s per "…"), listed in `out/vo_cues.srt`. Total length is 75.4 s; the calm pace adds about 5 s to the script's 70.

**Candidates.** (a) A sketchbook diary: each scene is a new page and pages turn. (b) One continuous page with the camera travelling. (c) A hybrid: four pages (street, bedroom/site cutaway, courtroom, rubble), with the bedroom page reused for scene 6 for continuity. **Chosen: (c).** Page turns mark time jumps. The bedroom page returns at night and tears open when the quake hits.

**Logline.** A tired man's proof of the tremors is erased in court. The night the real tremor comes, the vibrations nobody believed are what save him.

**Native moves.** Line before colour on every page (stroke-order draw-on). The tampering is a native un-drawing: an eraser rubs the ink graph flat, crumbs fall, and a grey wash floods the evidence. Red is the one coloured thing: the mesh, and nothing else in the film, is red.

**Shot list (camera).**
1. Wide street at dusk, then a lateral spring follow on Ramesh walking home. Page turn.
2. Bedroom cutaway: push in to his face on the pillow (moustache, specs, tired eyes), then pan to the drill rig next door, back to the glass rattling. Shake on "shakes".
3. Tilt down to the floor: the phone with a graph spiking night after night, and dated logs piling up. Page turn.
4. Courtroom wide, then push to the exhibit. On "tampered" the eraser flattens the graph. On "rewritten" the dates are crossed out and rewritten. On "officially" the permit is stamped NO ACTIVITY and a grey wash spreads over everything. Punch in to Ramesh's shocked face.
5. Pull back: three identical officials hand him the notice. DEFAMATION is stamped on that word.
6. The same bedroom at night (night wash, the notice on the table, same fan and glass). It shakes on "shakes". On "isn't" the ink goes violent, the page tears, and the city buckles. Page turn.
7. Rubble in the morning: rescuers with torches. A crane down below the rubble to Ramesh in his pocket.
8. Near black. Taps and small ink ripples.
9. The ripples rise. The flattened graph redraws itself spiking, and the eraser fails.
10. Red nodes, the mesh, bearings converge, the glow ("found"), then hand-lettered TREMORMESH and "Every Tremor Matters."

**Sound (no music).** Paper room tone; street ambience in scene 1; drill, pile-driver thumps, glass rattle and fan in scenes 2 and 6; pen, eraser, stamp and paper in court; rumble, crash and tear in scene 6; wind, debris and distant shouts in scene 7; true silence plus taps in scene 8; soft clicks for the nodes. Effects sit around -20 LUFS and duck under the narration windows.

**Text.** Handwritten on the page (dates, log entries, NO ACTIVITY, DEFAMATION, title). No burned-in subtitles in this cut.
